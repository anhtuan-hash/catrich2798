PART IV. TRUE / FALSE AND SHORT ANSWER
Read each statement and select True or False. Then answer the short questions.

1. A learner can improve vocabulary only by memorising isolated word lists.
A. True
B. False
Answer: B
Explanation: Vocabulary grows more effectively through context, retrieval, and repeated use.

2. Reviewing mistakes can help learners notice recurring language problems.
A. True
B. False
Answer: A
Explanation: Error review helps students identify patterns and adjust their strategies.

3. Give one reason why students should set a specific study goal.
Answer: A specific goal helps students focus their time and measure progress.
Rubric: Award full credit for one clear and relevant reason.

4. Rewrite the sentence using WHEN: The teacher arrived while the students were discussing the task.
Answer: The students were discussing the task when the teacher arrived.
Rubric: The rewritten sentence must preserve the original meaning and use correct tense forms.
