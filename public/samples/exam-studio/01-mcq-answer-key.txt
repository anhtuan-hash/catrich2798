PART I. MULTIPLE CHOICE
Choose the best answer A, B, C, or D.

1. The students _____ the project before the deadline yesterday.
A. finish
B. finished
C. have finished
D. are finishing

2. If the weather improves, the class _____ the outdoor activity tomorrow.
A. resumes
B. resumed
C. will resume
D. would resume

3. The teacher asked whether we _____ the instructions carefully.
A. read
B. had read
C. are reading
D. will read

ANSWER KEY
1. B
2. C
3. B

EXPLANATIONS
1. The time marker "yesterday" requires the past simple.
2. The first conditional uses will + bare infinitive in the main clause.
3. The past perfect shows that reading happened before the teacher asked.
