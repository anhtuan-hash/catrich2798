PART III. CLOZE AND WORD FORMATION
Complete each item with the best answer or the correct form of the word in brackets.

1. Effective revision requires a clear plan; _____, students may waste time on low-priority tasks.
A. otherwise
B. moreover
C. similarly
D. for example

2. The new timetable has improved student _____ in after-school activities. (PARTICIPATE)
Answer: participation

3. Teachers should encourage learners to use evidence _____ making a conclusion.
A. before
B. unless
C. despite
D. during

4. The workshop was highly _____ because every activity had a practical classroom purpose. (BENEFIT)
Answer: beneficial

ANSWER KEY
1. A
2. participation
3. A
4. beneficial

EXPLANATIONS
1. "Otherwise" introduces the negative result of not having a clear plan.
2. A noun is required after "student" in this phrase.
3. "Before" correctly expresses the sequence of actions.
4. An adjective is needed after "was highly".
