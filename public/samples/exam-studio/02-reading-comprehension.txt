PART II. READING COMPREHENSION
Read the following passage and choose the best answer.

PASSAGE 1. DIGITAL STUDY HABITS
Many secondary-school students now combine printed materials with digital tools when preparing for examinations. Online dictionaries, spaced-repetition applications, and interactive quizzes can make revision more flexible, but they are most effective when learners use them with a clear plan. Students who switch constantly between unrelated applications may remember less because their attention is repeatedly interrupted. By contrast, learners who set a specific goal, study for a fixed period, and review their mistakes tend to make steadier progress. Digital tools therefore support learning best when they are used purposefully rather than simply used more often.

1. What is the main idea of the passage?
A. Printed materials should be removed from schools.
B. Digital tools are useful when students use them purposefully.
C. Students should use as many applications as possible.
D. Online dictionaries are better than every other resource.

2. Why may some students remember less?
A. They study for a fixed period.
B. They review their mistakes.
C. Their attention is frequently interrupted.
D. They use printed materials.

3. The word "steadier" is closest in meaning to _____.
A. more consistent
B. more expensive
C. more complicated
D. more temporary

ANSWER KEY
1. B
2. C
3. A

EXPLANATIONS
1. The final sentence states the central message.
2. The passage directly links constant switching with interrupted attention.
3. "Steadier progress" means more consistent progress.
