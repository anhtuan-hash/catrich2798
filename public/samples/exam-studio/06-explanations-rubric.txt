PART V. QUESTIONS WITH EXPLANATIONS AND RUBRICS

1. Choose the option that best completes the sentence: The report _____ before it was sent to the school board.
A. checked
B. was checked
C. has checking
D. is check
Answer: B
Explanation: The subject "report" receives the action, so the passive voice is required.

2. Complete the sentence with the correct form of the word in brackets: The new policy was introduced to improve classroom _____. (SAFE)
Answer: safety
Explanation: A noun is required after "classroom" to name the condition being improved.

3. Explain why option C is incorrect in Question 1.
Sample answer: Option C is incorrect because "has checking" is not a valid English verb form and does not create a passive construction.
Rubric: 2 points for identifying the invalid verb form; 1 point for explaining the need for the passive voice.

4. Write an email of 80-100 words giving a classmate advice on preparing for an English test.
Sample answer: The response should include a greeting, at least two practical suggestions, supporting reasons, and an appropriate closing.
Rubric: Content 4 points; organisation 2 points; language accuracy 3 points; mechanics 1 point.
