ENGLISH TEST SAMPLE
Time allowed: 30 minutes

PART I. PRONUNCIATION
Choose the word whose underlined part is pronounced differently.

1. A. watched
B. needed
C. helped
D. stopped
Answer: B

PART II. GRAMMAR AND VOCABULARY
Choose the best answer.

2. By the time the meeting started, the principal _____ the revised plan.
A. approved
B. had approved
C. has approved
D. approves
Answer: B

3. The school introduced a mentoring programme _____ support new students.
A. so that
B. in order to
C. despite
D. because of
Answer: B

PART III. ERROR CORRECTION
Identify and correct the mistake.

4. The students were encouraged sharing their ideas during the discussion.
Answer: sharing → to share
Explanation: Encourage is followed by object + to-infinitive in the passive structure.

PART IV. WRITING

5. Write a paragraph of 100-120 words about one effective study habit.
Sample answer: A successful paragraph should introduce the habit, explain how it works, and provide a relevant example.
Rubric: Task response 3 points; vocabulary 2 points; grammar 2 points; coherence 2 points; mechanics 1 point.
